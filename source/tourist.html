Tourist Place

There are many beautiful places in Banyuwangi, but I will show the top ones.

Kawah Ijen

Mountain

Kawah Ijen is a volcanic crater in Banyuwangi, famous for its turquoise acidic lake and the rare blue fire phenomenon that appears at night. The dramatic landscape makes it one of the most iconic adventure destinations in East Java.

Fun Fact: Kawah Ijen is home to traditional sulfur miners who carry loads of 70–90 kg of sulfur down steep paths, showing incredible strength and endurance.

Alas Purwo National Park

Mystic

Alas Purwo National Park is one of the oldest forests in Java, often called a mystical place with deep cultural and spiritual value. The park features dense tropical jungle, savanna, and pristine beaches like Plengkung (G-Land), which is world‑famous among surfers. It’s also home to diverse wildlife such as banteng, deer, and exotic birds, making it a perfect blend of nature and adventure.

Pulau Merah

Sunset View

Pulau Merah is a beautiful beach in Banyuwangi, known for its reddish sand and stunning sunset panorama. The waves here are strong and consistent, making it one of the best surfing spots in East Java. With its natural charm and relaxed atmosphere, Pulau Merah is perfect for those who want to enjoy the evening sky or challenge themselves on the surfboard.

Best for: Sunset seekers & surfers.

De Djawatan Forrest

Photospot

De Djawatan is a mystical forest filled with giant trembesi trees that create a magical, cinematic vibe — often compared to scenes from “Lord of the Rings.” The shady atmosphere makes it a refreshing escape, while the unique tree formations turn it into a favorite photography spot. Affordable and easy to access, De Djawatan is ideal for visitors looking for a cheap trip with unforgettable photo opportunities.

That's all I can show for now. Beyond its tourist spots, Banyuwangi also serves traditional dishes that are equally tasty and memorable.
