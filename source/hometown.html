My Hometown

Patung Gandrung Sewu, Banyuwangi, Indonesia

Banyuwangi

Banyuwangi is my hometown, located at the eastern tip of Java Island, often called “The Sunrise of Java.” This region is geologically unique because it stands at the edge of Java’s eastern peninsula, where volcanic mountains meet the sea. Banyuwangi is surrounded by natural wonders such as Mount Ijen with its famous blue fire, the Alas Purwo National Park, and the Meru Betiri forest. The combination of mountains, forests, and coastline makes Banyuwangi not only a cultural hub but also a place where geology and nature shape daily life.

Rogojampi

Within Banyuwangi, I come from Rogojampi, a district that lies close to the heart of the regency. Rogojampi is known for its vibrant community and serves as a gateway for travelers arriving at Banyuwangi Airport. Growing up in Rogojampi gave me a strong connection to both the traditions of East Java and the natural environment around me. It is here, at the eastern tip of Java, that I learned to appreciate how geography and geology influence the identity of a place and its people.
